Scientific Value (SV) — Earned by keeping discoveries in your collection. Extract items for immediate shards, or keep them to accumulate SV for research. Each discovery adds its SV to your total while you hold it.
How the Tech Tree Works

Earning Scientific Value

SV comes from keeping discoveries in your collection — not from extracting them:

  • Keep items → Their SV adds to your total (shown above)
  • Extract items → You get shards but LOSE that item's SV

This is the core trade-off: immediate shards vs. long-term research progress.

How Research Works

  • Choose a tech and spend SV to begin research
  • Research takes real time (24h to 7 days depending on tier)
  • Only one research can be active at a time
  • Higher tiers require completing lower-tier techs first
  • Cancel anytime for a full SV refund

4 Branches

  • Exploration — Faster expeditions, better discoveries
  • Vehicles — Speed, cost efficiency, cargo capacity
  • Shard Generation — Solar output, dust resistance, night collection
  • Extraction — Higher shard yields, rare specimen bonuses

Scientist Bonuses

Your scientist has a primary branch (25% faster research, 20% cheaper) and may have a secondary branch (15% faster research). Research in their specialty branches to maximize efficiency.
