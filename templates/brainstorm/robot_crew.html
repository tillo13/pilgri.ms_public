Robot Crew Member

4th Crew Member — Built by the Scientist from Legendary Discoveries

April 2026 — Bug #1147 (P1) + #1230 (Buildable Robot)

The Pitch — Why a 4th Crew Member?

Right now, every colony has 3 crew: Captain (the player), Scientist (chosen at game start), and ARIA (the AI companion). The Robot is the 4th — and unlike the others, you build it.

Core fantasy: Your scientist takes legendary expedition discoveries — Crystal Sentinel, Quantum Crystal, Quantum Obelisk — and over weeks, constructs a robot companion from Martian materials. Think Bo from Star Wars building a droid from junk. This is the first time the scientist's Engineering stat actually matters.
Key questions for Luke:
  • Does the robot feel like a natural progression from the current 3-crew system, or does it need more narrative setup?
  • Should every captain eventually get a robot, or should it feel rare/achievement-based?
  • What's the robot's personality? Is it silent/mechanical, or does it develop character like ARIA?
Component Requirements — What You Need

The robot requires legendary discoveries as core components and rare discoveries as raw materials. This gives legendaries a PURPOSE beyond collection value.

Component Discovery Required Rarity Robot Function
Brain / AI Core Crystal Sentinel Legendary Intelligence, decision-making
Power Source Quantum Crystal Legendary Energy generation, endurance
Chassis / Body Quantum Obelisk Legendary Physical structure, durability
Raw Materials (various) Curiosity Gyroscope, Opportunity Processor, etc. Rare Wiring, joints, sensors, plating
Open questions:
  • How many rares needed? 3? 5? 10? More = longer grind but more satisfying payoff
  • Can you use ANY rare, or specific ones for specific parts?
  • What if you've already "sharded" (sold) your legendaries? Can you re-find them, or are you locked out?
  • Should there be a Shard cost on top of the discovery requirements?

Scientist Engineering Stat — Finally Matters

Engineering (ENG) directly controls build speed.
High-ENG scientists (Dr. Tom @ 48, Dr. Don @ 42) build significantly faster. Low-ENG scientists take much longer.
Build Duration by Engineering Stat
ENG 48 (Dr. Tom)~2 weeks total
ENG 35 (mid-range)~3-4 weeks total
ENG 20 (low)~6-8 weeks total
ENG 6 (Dr. Anna)~2+ months

Other Scientist Stats During Build

Stat Effect on Robot Build
Engineering Build speed — the primary lever
Analysis Less material waste — fewer rares needed to complete
Geology Identifies best components from inventory — quality bonus
Navigation Robot's efficiency once built — better at autonomous tasks
Open questions:
  • Can the scientist do other things while building? Or is robot construction exclusive (no expeditions while building)?
  • Should build phases be visible on the Colony page like Depot construction?
  • Can you speed up construction with Shards? (Luke flagged "way to speed up Depot build times" as P1)
  • What happens if you cancel mid-build? Lose materials? Partial refund?
What the Robot Does — Capabilities
The robot fills two critical gaps: autonomous trail building (Bug #1113) and ARIA Autopilot colony management (Bug #1145). It's the in-lore reason your colony runs while you're AFK.
Capability Description Related Bug
Autonomous Trail Building Robot builds trails while you're away. Speed based on robot level + Navigation stat from scientist who built it. #1113
Colony Management (AFK) Seeds ARIA Autopilot. In-lore reason colony keeps running when captain is offline. #1145
Expedition Support Can the robot go on expeditions? As a 4th crew member or as a solo autonomous scout? TBD
Depot Assistance Could speed up Depot build times (addresses Luke's P1 "Way to speed up Depot build times") #1265
Key design questions:
  • Does the robot have its own stats? If so, what determines them — the components used, the scientist who built it, or fixed values?
  • Can the robot be upgraded/leveled after construction?
  • Does the robot appear in the Crew page tab alongside Captain, Scientist, and ARIA?
  • Can the robot be damaged/destroyed? Does it need maintenance?
  • Robot personality — silent tool, or does it develop character? ARIA already fills the "AI companion" role

World 2 Connection — Martian Crew Member?

Luke's Signal Phase 2 feedback included a vision for World 2: after finding all 14 Earth signals, 14 new Martian heritage signals unlock — and one reward could be a new Martian crew member.

Design fork:
  • Option A: The Robot IS the 4th crew member (built from discoveries). Martian crew member is a 5th, unlocked much later via Signal World 2.
  • Option B: The Robot is the 4th. There is no 5th. The "Martian crew member" was the Robot all along — it was built from Martian materials.
  • Option C: Robot is a tool/companion (not a full crew member). Martian discovery from World 2 is the real 4th crew member.
This affects the entire crew system architecture. Needs a decision before implementation.
The Activation Moment — Cinematic

After weeks of construction, the robot activates. This should be a major narrative moment — on par with the Signal claiming cinematic or ARIA bond reveal.

Luke values game polish — character select grids, cinematic reveals, side-by-side comparisons. The activation should feel earned and epic, not just a toast notification.
