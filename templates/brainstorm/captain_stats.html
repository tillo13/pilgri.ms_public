Stats should be the backbone of every game system — expeditions, building, Signal detection, trail speed, robot construction — but right now they're mostly decorative.
Captain Stat Progression — How Stats Grow

From Bug #21: "Captain stats should increase based on gameplay activities — like baseball stats but as percentages."

Core concept: Stats grow by DOING things. Send more expeditions, your Exploration goes up. Build more trails, your Logistics increases. It's not XP — it's activity-driven progression.
Stat Grows From Example Progression
Leadership Colony management decisions, crew assignments, time on Mars +0.1 per Sol survived, +0.5 per crew mission completed
Strategy Successful expeditions, discoveries found, tech researched +0.2 per expedition, +1.0 per legendary found
Exploration Distance traveled, new landmarks visited, signals detected +0.01 per km traveled, +2.0 per new landmark
Logistics Trails built, Depot upgrades completed, resources managed +0.5 per trail segment, +1.0 per Depot upgrade
Charisma ARIA bond level, PilgrimBot conversations, Signal interactions +0.1 per ARIA chat, +2.0 per ARIA bond milestone
Key design questions:
  • Is progression linear or logarithmic? (Diminishing returns as stats get higher?)
  • Max cap stays at 75? Or does progression unlock higher caps (75 → 90 → 100)?
  • Should progression be visible? "Your Exploration increased to 34!" vs silent background growth?
  • Can stats decrease? Inactivity penalty? Or only go up?
  • Retroactive: should existing captains get credit for past activities?

EVA Suit Stat Bonuses — Existing but Broken

Bug #502 (Done) identified that EVA Suit upgrades have stat bonuses defined but never applied. The function get_effective_commander_stats() exists in shop_utils.py but is never called.

This is low-hanging fruit. EVA Suit stat bonuses are already defined in config. We just need to actually call the function. But we should wait for this brainstorm to confirm WHAT each stat does before activating bonuses to stats that currently do nothing.
Sequence question: Do we activate EVA Suit bonuses now (with existing undefined stats) or wait until stats have real gameplay effects? Activating now would give "+5 Leadership" when Leadership does nothing — feels hollow.
