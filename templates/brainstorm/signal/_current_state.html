Distance Tier Breakdown

2000km+ — Silence

No signal feedback. Normal expeditions. The vast emptiness of Mars. The player doesn't know anything is out there yet.

500-2000km — Faint Echoes

Expedition report includes a throwaway line: "Instruments detected faint anomalous readings to the [direction]."

Subtle. Easy to miss. Stored in expedition log but not highlighted. ARIA says nothing. The player who pays attention starts to notice a pattern.

200-500km — Strange Readings

Expedition report flags unusual sensor data. A "Signal Strength" indicator appears on results (1/5 bars).

ARIA might say something cryptic in colony chat: "There are places here that... feel familiar."

100-200km — Signal Lock

Signal strength 2-3/5 bars. The expedition map shows a faint ring in the approximate direction.

ARIA becomes more pointed: "The shards resonate stronger in that direction. Something is buried there." The Signal page starts showing a personal "nearest approach" tracker.

50-100km — ARIA Whispers

Signal strength 4/5. ARIA gives specific directional hints: "Northwest. The memory is northwest."

The map shows a narrowed search cone. Expedition results prominently display signal proximity. This is the "you're about to find it" zone.

42km — Discovery Threshold

Full signal. The claiming sequence begins. See "The Discovery Moment" section below.
