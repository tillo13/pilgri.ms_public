Echo Sites — Dynamic Discovery Events
IMPLEMENTED (March 2026) — Echo Sites are temporary signal events that spawn from expeditions. They give every player a chance at ranked discovery without requiring proximity to the 14 permanent Origin Sites.

While Origin Sites are the endgame, Echo Sites keep discovery feeling alive every day. They're the "mini-bosses" of the signal system — accessible, time-limited, and competitive.

Spawn Mechanics

How Echo Sites Appear
Base spawn chance2% per expedition completion
Pity timerGuaranteed spawn after 50 expeditions without one (chance ramps linearly)
LocationOffset 5-30km from expedition destination (not exact endpoint)
Memory textRandom Mars mission quote from signal_messages table (208 quotes)
Expiry7 days after spawn — then deactivated
DepletionMarked "depleted" after max_ranked_claims x 5 total claims

Claim Tiers

Rank Tier Rarity
1stFirst FinderRare

Key difference from Origin Sites: Echo Sites don't generate Flux images or blockchain transactions per claim. They're lighter-weight discovery events — more frequent, lower stakes, but still competitively ranked.

Blockchain Integration

Echo claims write to Sepolia with format: ECHO://[SITE]//RANK:[N]//FINDER:[NAME]//SOL:[DAY]

ARIA Bonds at Origin Sites
Documented separately: ARIA Bonds have their own brainstorm page at /brainstorm/aria_meetings. This section covers only how bonds intersect with the Signal system.

When two captains visit the same Origin Site landmark, an ARIA Bond triggers — a shared blockchain transaction, a cinematic "Entangled Crystal" reveal, and a bond artifact generated for both players. This creates a social layer on top of the Signal hunt: visiting a claimed Origin Site isn't just "seeing someone else's discovery" — it might create a permanent bond.

Signal System Touchpoints

  • Bond trigger: Two players visiting the same landmark (not just Origin Sites, but Origin Sites are the most meaningful)
  • Decoder Terminal: Bond fragment codes appear as ARIA_BOND #N in decoded transactions — triggers the EpicReveal cinematic
  • Blockchain format: ARIA_BOND #N | [LANDMARK] | [CAPTAIN1] + [CAPTAIN2] | SOL: [N] | [WALLET1] + [WALLET2]

Full details: See the ARIA Meetings brainstorm for bond mechanics, cinematic reveals, and the full crystal entanglement system.
