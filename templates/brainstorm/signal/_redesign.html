The Signal Page — What /signal Should Show

Currently /signal shows claimed/unclaimed nodes with cryptic IDs, echo sites, and a decoder. It's atmospheric but gives zero sense of personal progress. The page is identical for everyone.

What's Missing:
  • No personal proximity tracker ("Your closest approach to any origin site")
  • No signal strength per site (even vague Cold / Warm / Hot)
  • No expedition history showing signal-relevant trips
  • No directional hints for where to explore next
  • No sense that exploration is leading somewhere

Proposed Additions

Personal Signal Dashboard

A new section at the top of /signal showing YOUR progress: closest approach per active site (if within 2000km), number of signal artifacts collected, ARIA's current hint. Each player sees their own version.

Signal Strength Bars

Each unclaimed node shows 0-5 signal bars based on your closest approach ever. Creates a visual "I'm getting warmer on 3 sites" feeling. Bars fill as you explore closer.

Expedition Compass

"Your last 5 expeditions were all south. The strongest signal readings came from the northwest." Guides exploration without spoiling exact location.

Global Activity Feed (at scale)

"Captain [X] detected a signal echo near [region]" — shows other players are searching too. At scale, this creates competition dynamics.

  • Signal strength bars: exact numbers or fuzzy (Cold/Warm/Hot)?
Map Visualization — What Shows on Expeditions
IMPLEMENTED (March 2026): All 14 origin dots are now always visible on the expedition map as pulsing gold markers. Clicking an unclaimed node shows mysterious Signal-page-matching language instead of revealing mission details.

Current Map Popup Tiers

Unclaimed Node — No Proximity (default)

Shows NODE-XXXXXX (matching Signal page), signal strength (Strong/Faint/Fragmented from mission_status), and "Dormant. Awaiting contact." — no mission name, no distance, no hints.

Unclaimed Node — Within 1000km

Same as above plus a cryptic ARIA hint: "A distant echo. Too far to read, but... it's there."

Unclaimed Node — Within 500km

Stronger hint: "Something stirred during a recent journey... a faint resonance, ~XXX clicks out." Distance rounded to nearest 50km — close enough to guide, vague enough to not pinpoint.

Claimable — Within 42km

Mission name revealed for the first time. "Origin Site" label. Claim button. This is the moment.

Future Visual Layers (not yet built)

  • Signal Rings: Faint concentric circles that brighten as you approach. Never shows exact center until 42km.
  • Path Heat Map: Expedition paths colored by signal proximity — warmer = closer. Visual "treasure map."
  • Directional Indicators: After 200km tier, subtle arrow pointing toward signal source. Think BotW shrine sensor.
