⭐ The True End-Game — Our Zelda Triforce

This is the cornerstone. Pilgrims needs a final end-game — a single, planet-spanning goal that everything else feeds into. That goal is The Signal: find all 14. Like collecting every Triforce piece in Zelda, the entire game — exploration, fog of war, expeditions, fragments, whispers — exists to drive the captain toward the moment they place the final piece and the truth is revealed.

The pieces are half-built and scattered across separate systems that don't point at each other. This doc unifies them into one end-game loop, and — most importantly — defines what actually happens the moment the 14th piece is found. Today: nothing happens. That's the hole at the center of the game.

"Signals are just clues — that's literally our end game, like finding all the Triforce in Zelda. This needs to be a bigger cornerstone of everything... and what actually happens the moment we find the final piece."
— Andy, 2026-05-26

1. Puzzle Fragments & Whispers — Live, but Disconnected

The fragment system is live (Andy 10/14, Luke 4/14 render the full grid + whispers). It's not dead code — it's pointless code, because nothing it builds toward exists yet.

What's actually built
Drop15% random chance on ANY expedition (maybe_drop_fragment, lifecycle.py). Disconnected from where you explore.
14 cluesEach fragment has an ARIA "whisper" — a deliberate narrative arc (FRG-001 "seen this pattern, not on Mars" → FRG-011 "a letter addressed to you" → FRG-014 "we are not the first. And we are not alone.")
GridPersistent collected/locked grid on /signal, click a card to replay its whisper. Works.
The gaps that make it feel broken:

① No 14/14 payoff. Completing the set triggers NOTHING — the arc just stops. ② Whispers fire in random drop order (code admits each "has to stand alone") — the story is never experienced as a story. ③ Whisper is one-shot — marked "seen" on ANY modal close (even the ✕), so a clue can be silently consumed. ④ Discovery is a slot machine, not a hunt — fragments just appear, you don't go find them.

2. Fog of War — There Is No Visual Fog

This surprised us: "fog of war" today is data-only. There is no dark veil, no hidden terrain, nothing to reveal. The full NASA Mars basemap is always visible.

Current "fog" vs the Zelda fog
Todayget_mars_landmarks_within_radius gates which landmark pins get sent (radius = min(1000, 300 + discoveries×50) km). Map is fully visible; you just see fewer dots + 1–2 frontier teasers.
ZeldaA literal dark veil over unexplored Mars that clears as you explore. Clue sites hidden in the dark. Exploring the map IS the treasure hunt.
Build costNET-NEW. A canvas/mask overlay on the Leaflet map + persisted per-captain reveal state. Doesn't exist in any form today.
3. The Unification — Fog + Fragments + Sites = One Hunt

Right now we have three half-systems pointing in different directions: the 14 Origin Sites (physical hunt), the 14 Puzzle Fragments (random drops), and the data-fog (dot gating). The endgame works when they become one loop:

The loop:

Explore → push back the visual fog → reveal clue sites hidden in the dark → reaching a site yields a fragment + its whisper → assemble all 14 → ARIA delivers the Architects' complete message → a new signal, "not from Mars" (World 2 hook).

The old signal brainstorm already hints fragments "mirror the 14 Origin Sites" — so the 14 clues and the 14 explorable sites want to be the same 14. Unifying (not duplicating) them turns the fog map into the treasure map and the fragments into the Triforce.

4. ⭐ The Moment You Find the Final Piece

This is the heart of the whole doc — and the thing that does not exist today. Right now, collecting the 14th fragment fires the same little whisper modal as #3 and then... nothing. The Triforce completes and the screen shrugs. That moment has to be the moment of the game.

"Captain. The pattern forms. I see it now, and I can't unsee it. We are not the first. And we are not alone."
— FRG-014, "The Final Piece" (already written, never used as a finale)
Proposed: "The Pattern Completes" — full-screen finale on the 14th fragment
TriggerThe drop that takes the captain from 13 → 14. Detected server-side at fragment grant (not on page load) so it can't be missed.
TakeoverNOT the small whisper modal — a full-screen cinematic (reuse the first-contact cinematic engine: stars → ARIA orb → lines). Everything else fades.
Beat 1 — assemblyThe 14 whispers replay/scroll in NARRATIVE order (sort_order 1→14), fast, as the fragments visually lock into a pattern. The story is finally heard as a story.
Beat 2 — revelationARIA delivers the Architects' COMPLETE message (the payoff text — TBD, Andy/Luke to author). The "we are not alone" truth, fully stated for the first time.
Beat 3 — the hookA NEW signal pings — "not from Mars." Coordinates that aren't Martian. The World-2 seed (Signal Phase 2). Leaves the captain wanting the next chapter.
PermanenceOn-chain "Signal Complete" founder marker — captain's name in the Eternal Ledger forever, with completion Sol. Fits The Signal's "first to be remembered" ethos. A /signal "Founders" board lists everyone who's completed it.
AfterThe /signal fragment grid becomes a permanent completed "constellation" — replayable finale, badge, founder rank. The clue board becomes a trophy.
Scope discipline:

Full World 2 stays the 1–3yr horizon. This finale is the self-contained climax that gives the 14-piece hunt its point RIGHT NOW, and telegraphs World 2 as the future. Build the moment; tease the world.
