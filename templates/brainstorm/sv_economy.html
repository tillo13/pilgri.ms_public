Option C: Increase Extraction SV Bonus

Make Shard It All the SV engine. Currently extraction gives 15% of shard value as SV. If we bump that significantly, every extraction becomes meaningful for research.

15% (current) 150 SV 134 extractions

At 100%, every shard you extract is also 1 SV. Extract 20,000 shards worth of discoveries = 20,000 SV. Clean and intuitive: "Your scientist learns as much as they extract."

Q4 — Passive Floor: Is 120 SV/day Enough?

After the fix, Research Station Lv1 generates 5 SV/hr = 120 SV/day. This is the absolute floor for a player who does zero active play.

Scenario Daily SV Tier 1 Tech (5,000 SV) in Notes
Lv1 passive only (proposed) 120 42 days Total floor — zero active play
Lv3 passive only 288 17 days Once you upgrade station a bit
Lv5 passive only 600 8 days Mid-game station
Lv10 passive only 2,160 2.3 days Max station, no other actions

The intent is that passive alone is NOT sufficient — it's a floor that keeps you feeling forward progress, while active play (expeditions, milestones, sharding) is what actually drives tech tree progression. Does 42 days for the absolute laziest player feel right, or too punishing?
