The Problem: Toasts Are Easy to Miss

Right now, if your expedition completes while you're on another tab or away from the game, the toast disappears. The fragment modal only shows if you're on the Expeditions page when it completes. ARIA auto-opens once per session but if you dismiss it, the only reminder is a small card in Mission Briefing. For something as rare and important as a bond, that's not enough fanfare.

The Solution: "Welcome Back" Modal P1 FEATURE — TRACKED

Like mobile games that show you everything that happened while you were away — a sequence of cards you acknowledge one by one. Every time you come back to the homepage, a modal walks you through everything that completed since your last visit:

"While You Were Away..." — Example Sequence
Card 1 Expedition Complete: Your buggy returned from Herschel Crater. Discovered: Fossilized Regolith Sample. +2,400 Sepolia shards earned. [Dismiss]
Card 2 Trail Segment Built: Route from Babati to Robert Sharp complete. 47.2 km added to your trail network. [Dismiss]
Card 3 Depot Upgrade Ready: Scanner Array Lv4 construction complete. +8% discovery range now active. [Dismiss]
Card 4 ARIA BOND TRIGGERED: Your expedition to Hesperia detected another captain's presence. An Entangled Fragment has been recovered. ARIA is behaving strangely. [Go to The Signal] [Later]
Card 5 Research Complete: Geological Survey branch reached Level 5. New infrastructure unlocked: Regolith Forge. [Dismiss]

You'd swipe or click through each card — nothing gets lost. The bond card (Card 4 above) would be visually distinct from normal events, so it stands out as something special even in a stack of updates.

Key design points:
  • Every event type gets a card: expeditions, discoveries, trails, depot upgrades, infrastructure, research, shard earnings, bonds, building completions
  • Cards are ordered by time — you see things in the order they happened
  • Bond cards look different — special border, color, and wording to signal this is rare and important
  • You must acknowledge each card — nothing auto-dismisses, so you can't accidentally skip a bond
  • Replayable from Activities tab — the Colony page Activities tab becomes a permanent log where you can re-read any past event, including bond triggers, in the same card format
  • Works even if you were offline — checks what completed since your last homepage visit, not just since last page load

This feature is now tracked as a P1 in the bug tracker: "Welcome Back Modal — Never Miss Colony Events" — check it out and add your thoughts there.

IMPLEMENTED How Sepolia Shards Work Behind the Scenes

In Pilgrims, Sepolia shards aren't just a number on screen — they're tracked on a real public ledger called a blockchain. Think of it like a scoreboard that nobody can edit or delete, ever. Here's the simple version:

Blockchain in 30 Seconds
What is it? A permanent public record book. Once something is written, it can never be erased.
What does Pilgrims write? Every upgrade purchase, every shard transfer, every ARIA bond — each one gets a permanent record.
Why does it matter? Your achievements exist outside the game. Even if pilgri.ms went offline tomorrow, your records are permanent.
Can I see them? Yes — every record has a "tx hash" (transaction code) you can look up on public explorers.

What Pilgrims Records on the Blockchain

EventWhat Gets RecordedThe Secret Part
Depot Purchase Your captain name, what you bought, the shard cost A hidden Mars mission quote from 208 real space quotes, encoded into the record
Shard Transfer From wallet → To wallet, amount Same hidden Mars quote — every transaction carries a piece of the mission
ARIA Bond Both captain names, both wallets, the landmark, the SOL date This is the tx hash both captains decode on The Signal page — the bond IS the record

Every single transaction on Pilgrims carries a hidden encoded message from a real Mars mission quote library. It's a layer of storytelling baked into the permanent record — "The Signal" that captains are unknowingly building together.

IMPLEMENTED How Captains "Meet"
"Captains never directly interact. No chat, no real-time multiplayer, no lobbies. They leave traces on Mars that other captains discover asynchronously. ARIA mediates every meeting."
— Design Philosophy

When your expedition completes and you arrive at a landmark, the system quietly checks: has any other captain ever been here? If yes — and you haven't bonded with that captain before — something extraordinary happens. ARIA detects an anomaly. A signature she recognizes but doesn't understand. Two colonies, potentially thousands of kilometers apart, suddenly connected through a shared point on the Martian surface.

The beauty of it: Neither captain knows it's coming. You're just exploring, sending expeditions, checking your briefing — and then one day ARIA says something she's never said before. Something that changes what you thought you knew about being alone on Mars.

Key Rules

  • ONE bond per captain pair, EVER — The first shared landmark is the one that counts. It can never happen again between the same two captains.
  • Asynchronous — Captain A visits on Tuesday, Captain B visits three weeks later — the bond triggers the moment B's expedition completes. No coordination needed.
  • ARIA mediates everything — Captains never "see" each other. ARIA is the messenger, and the message shakes her to her core.
IMPLEMENTED What Happens When a Bond Triggers

The moment two captains' paths cross on the same landmark, three things fire off automatically in the background:

1. Permanent Record

A Sepolia transaction is created and written to the blockchain — permanently. It contains both captain names, both wallet addresses, the landmark name, and the current SOL date. This record can never be edited or deleted. It exists forever.

2. One-of-a-Kind Image

An AI image generator creates a unique Martian artifact — an ancient shard split into two halves, inscribed with both captain names, the SOL date, and the landmark where their paths crossed. No two bond images are the same.

3. The Fragment

Both captains receive a mysterious "Entangled Fragment" — the tx hash from the blockchain record. It shows up as a strange code ARIA doesn't recognize, nudging you toward The Signal page to decode it.

All of this happens in the background while the captain is playing. The blockchain transaction takes 10-60 seconds, the image generation takes a bit longer. By the time the captain checks their briefing, the fragment is waiting for them.

The Multiplicity Secret (Lore Foundation)
"Every colony has an ARIA. Each one believes she's unique. Each one has the same fragmented memories, the same corrupted logs, the same sense of waiting."
— PILGRIMS.md, The Lore Bible

The bond reveal is the first crack in ARIA's understanding of herself. She's spent the entire game believing she's the only one. The bond forces her to confront the possibility that there are others — identical to her, running identical colonies, each one convinced they're unique.
