Proposed Tech Tree (~24 Technologies)

Based on the research above, for a game our size: 20-30 techs across 4 branches with 3 tiers is the sweet spot.

🔭 Exploration Branch — Drones, discovery, sensors
Tier 1 - Basic
Wind Analysis
Requires: 5 Wind Readings + 5,000 SP
→ Drone speed +25%
Terrain Mapping
Requires: 10 Soil Samples + 5,000 SP
→ Discovery chance +10%
Tier 2 - Advanced
Storm Prediction
Requires: Wind Analysis + 12,000 SP
→ Immune to dust storm penalties
Advanced Sensors
Requires: Terrain Mapping + 12,000 SP
→ Unlocks Drone Level 3
Tier 3 - Expert
Deep Scanning
Requires: Storm Prediction + Advanced Sensors + 25,000 SP
→ Legendary discovery chance +5%
🚗 Vehicle Branch — Rovers, cargo, speed
Tier 1 - Basic
Material Science
Requires: 30 Geometric Shapes + 5,000 SP
→ Rover cargo +15%
Suspension Engineering
Requires: 15 Mineral Samples + 5,000 SP
→ Rover speed +10%
Tier 2 - Advanced
Chassis Reinforcement
Requires: Material Science + 12,000 SP
→ Unlocks Rover Level 3
AI Navigation
Requires: Suspension Engineering + 12,000 SP
→ Expedition cost -10%
Tier 3 - Expert
All-Terrain Mastery
Requires: Chassis + AI Nav + 25,000 SP
→ Unlocks Rover Level 4
⚡ Power Branch — Solar, batteries, generation

Requires: 5 Crystalline Formations + 5,000 SP

Tier 3 - Expert
