We looked at how major strategy games structure their tech trees to inform our approach:

Game Total Techs Structure Key Mechanic
Civilization 6 ~118 (67 tech + 50 civics) 2 trees, 9 eras "Eureka" - mini-quests give 50% boost
Stellaris ~400 3 areas, 13 subcategories, 6 tiers Card shuffle randomizes available research
Factorio ~200 Multiple paths, progressive Science packs as currency, infinite late-game
Age of Empires IV ~50-70 per civ By building type, 4 ages Civilization-specific trees
Dawn of Man ~30-40 Adaptive unlock Knowledge from actions (hunt, build, craft)
Key Design Principles We Found:
  • Branching factor ~1.5 - Civ V uses 1.49. Too many branches = decision paralysis.
  • "One choice = no choice" - Every tech should feel useful, not just a prerequisite.
  • Thematic pillars - Organize around 3-5 themes (for us: Exploration, Vehicles, Power, Extraction).
  • Dawn of Man insight - "You get knowledge for doing things" = Luke's idea of science points from extraction!

Our scale recommendation: For an indie game our size, 20-30 techs is manageable. Civ's 118 or Stellaris's 400 would be overkill.
