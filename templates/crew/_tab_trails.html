How trails work — the 4 chains, colors, math click to expand

Every captain has exactly 4 trails — one per cardinal direction (N / E / S / W). Each is a chain of landmark hops from your base out to your antipode — the landmark on the opposite side of Mars. All 4 chains terminate at the same antipode, just routed through different hemispheres.

⬆ N CHAIN — solid white
Routes via the North Pole
➡ E CHAIN — cyan, long-dashed
Routes east through the equator
⬇ S CHAIN — magenta, dotted
Routes via the South Pole
⬅ W CHAIN — black, dash-dot
Routes west through the equator

Each chain uses a high-contrast color (white / cyan / magenta / black) PLUS a unique dash pattern, with a dark halo, so colorblind captains can tell them apart by shape and luminance — not just color.

On the map: each chain shows as a dashed line in its signature color tracing the FULL route from base to antipode. Bright thick segments = what you've built. The color circle at the end of each line is the antipode beacon (same landmark, four routes). Click + Build / drone cron / ARIA Resonance all advance whichever direction is set as active (highlighted in Top Trails below).

Per-session km formula
0.75 × crew_mult × scanner × eva_suit × consumable × (duration/60)

Equipment (stacks):
Scanner +1-10%/lv · EVA Suit +5%/lv · Consumables +6-15%
Passive (24/7) — runs every 30 min via cron
Maintenance Drone: Lv1 0.5 km/hr → Lv3 1.0 km/hr
Mining Drone: Lv1 1.5 km/hr → Lv7 8.0 km/hr
Robot Crew: dial[exploration] × stage × Robotics Lab × scientist Nav

Why this matters: chains are the only trail system. ~10,500–12,000 km per direction, 18–20 segments. At ~20–25 km/day baseline you finish one chain in 12–18 months — all four is the grand circumnavigation. Speed it up with depot upgrades, scientist Engineering stat, captain Logistics stat, and tech research. Off-chain expeditions get no trail bonus.
